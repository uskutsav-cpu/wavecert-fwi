# Contribution assessment: stronger comparators required

No specific new certification contribution is established yet. The latest primary-source review narrows what could reasonably count as one.

[Qian et al. (2017)](https://kiwi.oden.utexas.edu/papers/multifidelity-optimization-adaptive-reduced-model-qian-grepl-veroy-willcox.pdf) already combine reduced-model cost/gradient error estimates, adaptive enrichment, parameter regularization and runtime/overhead comparisons. [Keil et al. (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1051/m2an/2021019) include constrained reduced optimization and corrected-gradient distinctions. [Macedo and Bueno (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40314-026-03794-2) provide adaptive state/adjoint accuracy control and inexact gradient/BFGS applications. Their assumptions and operators differ, but adding a prior derivative, box constraints or adaptive stopping is not a generic novelty claim here.

The implementation's cost comparison also needs care. Its paid reference-merit calculation already supplies reusable factors and forward fields. Increasing grid size does not automatically let certification claim the cost of avoiding an exact factorization. The relevant comparison includes the **additional derivative work** after that paid calculation, with source/receiver geometry, collocation and available fields held explicit. See the [cost model](cost_model.md). A cold exact-gradient comparison against a warmed reduced model would not establish inversion benefit.

A plausible, still-unproved investigation is a predictive operating region: when can a model/direction-specific error budget stop missing wave-adjoint work early and improve the total returned recovery/cost tradeoff? It must compare optimized exact/cached routes and adaptive inexact Krylov controls, include verification and setup, and preserve useful recovery on physically accepted independent-model cases. Elementary addition of an exact prior derivative to an existing directional error bound is not new theory. The prior term can widen or shrink the allowable data-error budget and is zero-gradient at initialization.

This is an AI-authored research advisory, not the student's final question, complete design, interpretation or priority claim. Recovery benefit is a prerequisite still missing from the current studies. The [source-backed advisory](../results/contribution_prior_art_review/advisory_01.json) records nine primary sources, their inspected scope, overlap, specific falsifiers and remaining evidence requirements. Current model priors, reciprocal rank bounds, archive volume and passing CI do not establish novelty or national readiness.
