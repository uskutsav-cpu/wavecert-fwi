# Continuing scientific research: full objective remains open

The new active goal is to establish matched-total-cost inversion benefit,
realistic wave physics across independent geological environments, a specific
contribution beyond existing certification methods, and independent reproduction
and scientific defense with accurate assistance disclosure. **None is declared
complete merely because code/tests pass.** The previous software-strengthening
pass is a foundation, not completion of this scientific goal.

The [360-run paid-merit engineering comparison](paid_merit_engineering_benchmark.md)
has completed with two passed independent post-audits. Its strong exact L-BFGS-B
baseline returns both objective targets in 48/48 attempts, versus 30/48 for the
certified arm. An additive post-outcome baseline diagnostic shows certified

This establishes a limitation, not the requested positive practical result.
No neural/POD inference occurs in this classical cached-field comparison.

bounds covering literally and no false certifications. This advances a specific
numerical-reproduction subrequirement, not all-trajectory replay or the four
scientific goals as a whole.

The new [seven-case Devito boundary diagnostic](devito_boundary_verification.md)
has executed and been audited. In its fixed homogeneous fixture, 20/40 damping

## Work now implemented or in controlled development

- A fixed untimed refinement probe retained all 144 comparisons / 3,456 blocks on
  all 8 reused validation slices at 24/49/99 interior grids. It interpolates old
  velocities and basis columns, so it is not native high-resolution geology.
  Fixed repair restores strong gradient quality; pure coarse rank 64 with four repairs
  certifies 8/8 at every grid. Rank 64 with POD and two repairs fails one 99-grid case.
  See `results/refinement_feasibility`, including every negative and scope limit.
- New `DeflatedDirectionCertifier` implements the pure coarse/repair proposal
  with the existing three-term physical-gradient bound. Its 18 synthetic tests
  and separate AI review cover dense sine-reference updates, complex/repeated
  receivers, FD/finite-grid bounds, all cost units, content caches, unavailable
  cases, immutable outputs, numerical failures and zero-proposal abstention.
  This is no claim of novel repair theory or project-data performance for the
  new implementation before its own frozen study.
- A stronger paid-merit oracle is integrated: retain accepted exact
  forward fields/receiver maps and LU factors across rejected trials; charge
  only the genuinely additional exact gradient work. The old optimizer's
  repeated forward solves cannot be the strongest baseline.
- A norm-free coarse/DST right-GMRES reference is integrated with actual
  true-residual stopping, explicit nonconvergence, warm starts, forward/adjoint
  checks and actual RHS/FFT/Arnoldi work. It avoids unused certificate SVDs.
  Tolerance-converged fields are numerical approximations, not exact arithmetic.
- A cached-merit-conditioned certificate is integrated. Paid U or
  receiver-map fields can reduce missing gradient work, but their measured
  residuals must remain in the bound. A numerical solve is never set exact by
  fiat. Both forward and receiver-coded routes, duplicate receiver weights and
  residual coherence must be checked against independent fine gradients.
